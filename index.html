Who We Are

Built on Compassion and Selfless Service

The Naalex Foundation (NAALEX) is a non-profit and non-political organization driven entirely by a commitment to selfless service. Operating across the entire Republic of Cameroon, we strive to reach the most vulnerable members of our society.

Headquartered in Buea, South West Region, our team works tirelessly to bridge the gap for those struggling with poverty, illness, and lack of community care.

Our Core Mission

Objectives That Drive Us Forward

Elderly Care

To take care of elderly people coming from very poor backgrounds, ensuring dignity and comfort in their later years.

Terminal Illness Support

To assist individuals suffering from terminal diseases with specialized care and compassionate aid.

Orphans & Vulnerable Youth

To assist orphans and vulnerable children, securing their developmental and basic livelihood requirements.

Supporting the Demented

To assist individuals living with dementia, offering specialized support structures and relief to caregivers.

Join Our Cause

Membership Criteria

Membership to the Naalex Foundation is open to all individuals who share our vision and are dedicated to selfless service:

  • Open Minds: Individuals with free or open minds willing to assist those mentioned in the objectives of the association.
  • Gender Inclusion: Open to both male and female participants.
  • Age Requirement: Open to persons aged 18 years and above.

Sustainability

Sources of Funds & Support

To successfully execute our community programmes, projects, and outreach objectives, the Naalex Foundation relies on transparent, sustainable channels of funding:

  • Members Registration & Annual Dues: Contributions from dedicated members.
  • Donations & Legacies: Generous contributions from national and international partners.
  • Fundraising Activities: Public and private initiatives driving project support.
  • Government Subventions & Grants: Institutional backing for official initiatives.
